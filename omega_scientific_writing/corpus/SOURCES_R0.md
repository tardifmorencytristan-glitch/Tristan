# Official source anchors — R0

These are authority anchors for rules encoded in R0. They are pointers, not copies of full documentation.

## Polytechnique Montréal

- Thesis/dissertation page: https://etudiant.polymtl.ca/etudes/programmes/etudes-superieures/programmes-etudes/memoires-et-theses/1000
- R0 verified public facts: current standards must be followed; the presentation guide is the institutional reference; submission uses a verification checklist; use of a Word or LaTeX template is mandatory; article-based theses are supported under specific procedures.

## LaTeX Project

- Documentation: https://www.latex-project.org/help/documentation/
- Tagged PDF guidance: https://tagging-project.latex-project.org/documentation/usage-instructions
- R0 verified public facts: accessible PDF has dedicated current guidance; current releases are recommended; LuaLaTeX is preferred for new documents in current tagging guidance.

## IEEE

- Journal article structure: https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/create-your-ieee-journal-article/create-the-text-of-your-article/structure-your-article/
- R0 verified public facts: abstract up to 250 words and self-contained; methodology should be detailed enough for reproduction; results/discussion should not exaggerate conclusions and should acknowledge limitations.

## Elsevier

- LaTeX instructions: https://www.elsevier.com/researcher/author/policies-and-guidelines/latex-instructions
- R0 verified public facts: Elsevier supplies the `elsarticle` class; reference style depends on the target journal; submission packaging constraints may differ from local source-tree organization.

## Rule

Institution/journal instructions override generic style preferences. External writing books and exemplars are advisory unless a target authority explicitly adopts them.
