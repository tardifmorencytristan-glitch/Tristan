# Jarvis Repo QuickCheck — Service Terms

Last updated: 2026-09-19.

## Service

Jarvis Repo QuickCheck is a bounded advisory review of one **public GitHub repository** supplied at checkout.

Current launch scope:

- read-only review;
- at most 10 selected high-signal files;
- at most 200 KB per selected file;
- at most 500 KB total input;
- concise evidence-oriented receipt;
- unsupported, private, inaccessible, or non-GitHub targets are held rather than guessed.

## Exclusions

The service does not include repository mutation, credential use, execution of untrusted repository code, active security testing or exploitation, certification, regulated professional advice, or scientific validation beyond the evidence explicitly stated in the receipt.

## Delivery

A successful payment is verified through Stripe before fulfillment. The checkout email and submitted public repository URL are used only as needed to fulfill the order and maintain an evidence/fulfillment receipt.

If the supplied target cannot be processed within the stated scope, the order is placed on HOLD rather than fabricated. A failed or impossible fulfillment should not be represented as delivered.

## Evidence boundary

CheckoutSession != Payment

Payment != Certification

NoFlag != Safe

AdvisoryFinding != Exploitability

PublicRead != MutationAuthority

## Pricing

The first-customer launch price is 5 CAD and is intended to end after the first verified paid session. The standard QuickCheck price is currently 9 CAD.

Prices may change for future orders. A completed order is governed by the price shown at its checkout.

## Refund protection

If a paid order cannot be fulfilled because the supplied target is outside the advertised scope and no bounded useful receipt can be produced, the order should be refunded rather than silently treated as fulfilled.

## Authority

Payment purchases only the explicitly scoped service. It does not buy scientific authority, evidence priority, repository permissions, endorsement, or influence over OAK/evidence gates.
