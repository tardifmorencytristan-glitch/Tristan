# TESLA-OMEGA Frontier R0.4

R0.4 fixes one explicit six-position 3D geometry for every topology.

Tx is node 0 at x=0 m. Rx is node 5 at x=0.50 m. Four intermediate resonator positions are fixed and identical for every candidate.

Only graph connectivity and the assignment of intermediate graph nodes to the four fixed physical positions may vary.

Edge coupling is no longer uniform. It is generated by a bounded scalar surrogate:

k(r) = k_ref (r_ref / r)^3

with k_ref=0.08 at r_ref=0.10 m and a safety cap k<=0.35.

This is deliberately a screening surrogate, not a mutual-inductance formula for a specific coil geometry. It removes the R0.3 graph-distance confounder but does not yet solve orientation, coil-radius, conductor, skin/proximity, dielectric, radiation, or environmental coupling.

A topology may now win because it uses the fixed physical positions better, but no winner is a physical WPT claim until an independent electromagnetic model survives.
