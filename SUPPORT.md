# Support Tristan Open Research & Tools

If Tristan's public tools, experiments, documentation, or research infrastructure are useful to you, you can optionally support their continued development.

**Voluntary contribution:**
https://donate.stripe.com/4gM7sL5CAg7wdwz3v283C07

The amount is chosen by the contributor. The current Stripe page accepts contributions starting at **5 CAD**, with **25 CAD** prefilled.

## What support means

Contributions help fund development, maintenance, testing, documentation, compute, and public experimentation around Tristan tools and research infrastructure.

A contribution:

- does **not** purchase a specific product or service;
- does **not** promise financial return;
- does **not** guarantee a scientific result;
- does **not** grant priority, authority, repository access, or influence over evidence gates;
- does **not** change OAK, evidence, validation, or scientific standards.

Paid services, when offered separately, have their own explicit scope and fulfillment terms.

## Boundaries

`Contribution != Investment`
`Contribution != ScientificValidation`
`Payment != Authority`
`Payment != PriorityEvidence`
`Support != GuaranteedDeliverable`
